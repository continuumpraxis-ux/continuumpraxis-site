About

Alex Koenig

Founder. Producer. One-person studio with an AI workforce.

Continuum Praxis is the studio I use to install marketing systems around brands that already have the hard part — the craft, the years, the product — and still do not have a trustworthy path from attention to a booked action to a community that remains.

There is no account team. I work inside the stack: paid and organic insights, analytics, CRM, inboxes, class or consult infrastructure, partnership briefs, and the unglamorous work of keeping spam out of the pipeline. Bots draft, file, and research against a shared knowledge base. I stamp. Nothing client-facing ships unattended.

The studio takes work across industries. It is not the operating layer of any single business. Public cases use category labels only.

The name is literal. Continuum: the work does not reset every Monday. Praxis: theory has to become an operating week.

How the studio works

Precision over volume

A fifteen-page dashboard that cannot name a next action is not marketing. A small test that names the winning message is.

Function first

Sites, decks, and ads exist to move a specific job. Decorative work that does not change the result is waste.

Owned infrastructure

Rented platforms are a door. The brand should own the house — hub, classes, CRM, measurement.

Human-approved AI

Bots are labor. Approval is how a single operator keeps control of direction without pretending to do every draft by hand.

Currently

A studio, not a vertical shop.
