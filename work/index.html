Medical Weight Loss Clinic

Assignment: connect existing clinical expertise to audience, education, conversion infrastructure, and community — and stop treating platform vanity as revenue.

What the system built

Content, operations, and community in the same quarter.

Positioning

People search for an explanation before they search for a protocol. Category products are becoming infrastructure. The differentiator is investigating the story before prescribing the next step.

Content engine

Comparison education, approved testimonials, B-roll, anniversary narrative (trust → education → human connection → authority → event → conversion). Direct promotion stays a minority share.

Paid vs organic

On a niche professional account, follower growth showed up when pieces were amplified. Organic posts in the same window produced little or no follow growth. Content finds winners. Paid creates the reach those winners need.

Social acquisition on the site

In a social cut of analytics: Facebook 154 sessions and 19 key events. Instagram 14 sessions and 3 events. YouTube 2 sessions and 3 events — a tracking question, not a victory lap. Sessions are visits.

CRM and telephony

Messenger and Instagram into a shared inbox. Comment automation. Random-caller pipeline so spam stops contaminating opportunity reports. Voicemail cleanup without touching the public business line.

Community

Rented social group kept as an entry point, not the whole house. Owned hub scoped for classes, education, and progress tools. Live classes treated as product, with notes and post-class surveys.

Case 02

Beauty Clinic

Assignment: build a marketing system around consult demand without turning the brand into before-and-after theater.

Positioning

Education and clinician voice ahead of procedure catalogs. The outcome sold is a trustworthy path to a consult, not a promised transformation.

System work

Content architecture, site jobs, and conversion routing for Beauty Clinic. Public metrics are withheld until they are reconciled to the business system and cleared for reuse.

No spend, reach, or booking figures are published for this workstream on this site.

Partnership posture

Community, not a sponsorship pitch.

Distribution conversations are framed as education, shared experience, and measurable activation — not as a request for tickets or a lead grab. Measurement is defined before launch: landing page → key action → CRM.

No partnership on this page is presented as a closed deal.
